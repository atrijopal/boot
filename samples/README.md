# Samples

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ami_*.flac`, `12_min.flac`, `15_min.flac`, `12_min_office_planning.flac` | excerpts of public AMI Meeting Corpus recordings (CC BY 4.0), 5 to 15 minutes. Upload any of them to try the workspace |
| `7_min_demo_2_lcd_budget_and_production_plan.flac` | **7 min** (420 s), **second demo clip**: AMI IS1008d, 11:00 to 18:00 of a final design meeting. Four voices (about 43%, 23%, 18% and 15% of the words), about 1,290 words, very little overlapping chatter. The prototype is presented and the component costs are checked against the budget; the group finds the LCD display does not fit the budget and the project manager commits, in the first person, to negotiate with the vendors and to speak to management. Figures such as euro costs and a two-million-unit market appear. Three human-marked decisions fall inside it. Real reference transcript included | AMI, CC BY 4.0 |
| `8_min_demo_remote_design_review.flac` | **8 min** (480 s), **the demo clip**: AMI ES2006c, 24:00 to 32:00 of a design meeting. Four active voices (about 34%, 29%, 20% and 16% of the words), about 1,760 words. The industrial designer, interface specialist and marketing expert present and argue over the display and scroll wheel, power source (hand dynamo and solar ruled out, simple battery favoured), case shape and material, and colours. Nine human-marked decisions fall inside it, and the human summary's tasks (look and feel, user interface, product evaluation, prototype) belong to this meeting. Real reference transcript included | AMI, CC BY 4.0 |
| `7_5_min_remote_cost_tradeoffs.flac` | **7.5 min** (450 s) of AMI ES2002d (5:30 to 13:00): the team checks the remote's production cost against the target and decides which features to keep or drop. Seven human-marked decisions fall inside it | AMI, CC BY 4.0 |
| `7_5_min_banana_remote_evaluation.flac` | **7.5 min** of AMI IS1004d (2:30 to 10:00): the prototype (a yellow banana-shaped remote) is presented and rated against the marketing criteria. Six human-marked decisions fall inside it | AMI, CC BY 4.0 |
| `7_5_min_remote_design_choices.flac` | **7.5 min** of AMI ES2016d (14:30 to 22:00): final choices for energy source, chip and case, with prices. Six human-marked decisions fall inside it; the speech is the least overlapped of the four | AMI, CC BY 4.0 |
| `7_5_min_project_kickoff_introductions.flac` | **7.5 min** of AMI IS1008a (7:30 to 15:00): a kick-off where the group introduces itself, roles are clarified, and the project manager asks for a plan and a functional design. Speakers address one another by name ("Christine"), so it is the one to try for speaker names; two human-marked decisions fall inside it | AMI, CC BY 4.0 |
| `jfk.flac` | a short speech clip for a quick smoke test |
| `bad/` | an empty file, a text file renamed to audio and similar: they should fail with a clear message, not a crash |
| `references/*.reference.json` | human transcripts with speakers (and, for the scenario meetings, a human summary and timed decision annotations) for the 12/15-minute clips and the four 7.5-minute clips, built by `scripts/make_reference.py`; `scripts/evaluate.py JOB REFERENCE` scores a processed meeting against one (word error rate, speaker accuracy) |
| `example_meetings/example-es2010b/` | a real record this application produced from `ami_ES2010b_600s-900s.flac`, with its audio. On first start it is copied into `outputs/` and shown in the interface as an **Example**; it can be opened and exported but not re-processed |
| `example_outputs/example-es2010b/` | the files the Export button produces for that example (raw and refined transcripts, report, structured record, action items, email summary, package) |
| `example_outputs/12_min_office_planning/` | outputs of an earlier run on the office-planning clip, with the five required outputs; they show the formats and are not a benchmark |

New recordings always go through the real pipeline; nothing here is returned in place of a result.

The four `7_5_min_*` clips were chosen from the public annotations as 7.5-minute windows with the most human-marked decisions and at least three active speakers, from meetings not used by the other samples. They have not been run through the pipeline yet, so what the pipeline finds in them is not characterised. AMI's scenario meetings mostly use roles rather than personal names; for real first names use `12_min_office_planning.flac`.
