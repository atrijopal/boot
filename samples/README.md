# Sample audio

| File | What it is | Source / license |
|---|---|---|
| `jfk.flac` | 11 s speech clip (not a meeting): quick smoke test | US public domain (from the openai/whisper test files) |
| `ami_ES2008a_120s-420s.flac` | 5 min excerpt of a 4-person design-team meeting | AMI Meeting Corpus, CC BY 4.0 |
| `ami_ES2002a_180s-480s.flac` | 5 min excerpt of another design-team meeting | AMI Meeting Corpus, CC BY 4.0 |
| `ami_IS1000a_240s-540s.flac` | 5 min excerpt of an interactive-scenario meeting | AMI Meeting Corpus, CC BY 4.0 |
| `example_outputs/*.json` | A saved Phase 1 result (the JSON the page offers for download) | generated by this app from an AMI excerpt |
| `bad/*` | Broken inputs: empty, silent, corrupt, text posing as mp3, `.txt`, too short | generated, for error-handling checks |

AMI meetings are scripted-scenario meetings where participants play roles in a product-design team.
Attribution: Carletta, J. et al., "The AMI Meeting Corpus" — https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/ (CC BY 4.0).
Excerpts are the headset-mix audio converted to 16 kHz mono FLAC and trimmed; the AMI sets are the only
meeting-style audio here and are not domain-specific to the pipeline (it discovers terms per recording).
Run any of them: `python scripts/run_phase1.py samples/<file>`.
