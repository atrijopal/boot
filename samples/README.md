AMI meetings are scripted-scenario meetings where participants play roles in a product-design team.
Attribution: Carletta, J. et al., "The AMI Meeting Corpus" — https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/ (CC BY 4.0).
Excerpts are the headset-mix audio converted to 16 kHz mono FLAC and trimmed; the AMI sets are the only
meeting-style audio here and are not domain-specific to the pipeline (it discovers terms per recording).
Run any of them: `python scripts/run_phase1.py samples/<file>`.

Notes
- The AMI meeting series are named a, b, c, d for successive meetings of the same design team. Later ones tend to contain more decisions and tasks than the first.
- Only the two ES2002a excerpts and ES2008a/IS1000a (first excerpts) have been run through the pipeline so far. The four newest 7.5-minute excerpts and the three 5-minute excerpts in `new/` were added for testing and have not been run, so their contents are not yet characterised.
- Each full AMI meeting is 24 to 43 minutes. They can be downloaded from the AMI corpus site for long-meeting tests.
